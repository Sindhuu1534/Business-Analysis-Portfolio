# Canon Inc. Strategic Business Analysis  

**Tools:** Excel, PowerPoint, Financial Ratio Analysis, Strategic Frameworks  
**Skills:** SWOT, PESTEL, Porter’s Five Forces, Value Chain, Financial Analysis  

### Project Overview  
Conducted an in‑depth strategic analysis of Canon Inc., evaluating its market position, financial performance, and diversification efforts. Developed actionable strategies for competitive advantage and long‑term growth.  

### Highlights  
- Performed SWOT, PESTEL, and Five Forces analysis to assess market dynamics.  
- Built financial ratio models to study profitability, liquidity, and leverage.  
- Proposed diversification into medical imaging and semiconductor markets.  
- Delivered a 40‑page strategic report aligning R&D investment with market transformation goals.
