# Clipboard Health Shift Offers Analysis  

**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI  
**Skills:** Data Analytics, Dynamic Pricing, Workforce Optimization  

### Project Overview  
Analyzed per‑diem shift data for Clipboard Health to uncover trends in worker engagement, pay‑rate sensitivity, and fulfillment efficiency. Proposed AI‑driven dynamic pricing and retention strategies to improve operational profitability.  

### Highlights  
- Evaluated correlation between pay rates and shift‑claim rates across time slots.  
- Built Excel dashboards to visualize booking efficiency and NCNS (no‑call‑no‑show) patterns.  
- Recommended smart pricing algorithms to enhance shift fulfillment by 15 %.  
- Created actionable KPI metrics for retention and revenue growth.
